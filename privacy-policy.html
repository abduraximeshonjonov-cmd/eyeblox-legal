Privacy Policy

Color Block: Eyeblox · 19 October 2026

In short

Color Block: Eyeblox does not ask for or itself collect any personal data. There is no sign-up and no analytics. Ads are shown only when you choose to tap "Watch video" (for a bolt or coins).

Data stored on your phone

Your game progress (levels unlocked, coins, powers, characters bought, wallpapers and widgets, settings) is stored only on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Coin packs, bolt packs and the Emperor (VIP) are sold through Google Play. Payment is handled by Google; your card and account details never reach us. The game only learns whether a purchase succeeded. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Rewarded videos (ads)

Videos are shown by Google AdMob. When a video is requested, AdMob may use some information about your device (advertising ID, IP address, device type) under its own policy. Users in Europe are asked for consent first. Details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Permissions

The game uses two permissions: SET_WALLPAPER, only when you tap "Set as wallpaper", and INTERNET, only for rewarded videos. The home screen widget shows the progress stored on your phone.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Sozlamalar \u2192 "Progressni tozalash" tugmasi yoki o'yinni o'chirib tashlash telefoningizdagi barcha o'yin ma'lumotlarini o'chiradi. Google Play'dagi xaridlar tarixi Google hisobingizda qoladi.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be published on this page and in the game, with a new date.
