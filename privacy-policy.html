Privacy Policy

Color Block: Eyeblox · 19 October 2026

In short

Color Block: Eyeblox never asks you for personal information such as your name, email or phone number, and there is no sign-up. Only anonymous usage statistics are collected, to make the game better (details below). Ads are shown only when you tap "Watch video" yourself (for a bolt or coins).

Data stored on your phone

Your game progress (levels unlocked, coins, powers, characters bought, wallpapers and widgets, settings) is stored only on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Usage statistics

To make the game better, anonymous statistics are collected through Google Analytics for Firebase: which levels are started, won or lost, how much time was left, what powers and coins are used for, as well as the device model, Android version, game language and the approximate country derived from the IP address. Each installation gets a random identifier; your name, email, phone number and advertising ID are not collected. The data is sent encrypted to Google's servers, used only to improve the game, never sold and never used for advertising. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Coin packs, bolt packs and the Emperor (VIP) are sold through Google Play. Payment is handled by Google; your card and account details never reach us. The game only learns whether a purchase succeeded. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Rewarded videos (ads)

Videos are shown by Google AdMob. When a video is requested, AdMob may use some information about your device (advertising ID, IP address, device type) under its own policy. Users in Europe are asked for consent first. Details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Permissions

The game uses two permissions: setting a wallpaper (SET_WALLPAPER), only when you tap "Set as wallpaper", and the internet (INTERNET), for the usage statistics and the rewarded videos. The home-screen widget shows the progress kept on your phone.

Children

The game suits all ages. We do not ask anyone, children under 13 included, for personal information such as a name or email, nor collect it; only the anonymous statistics described above are collected.

Deleting your data

Sozlamalar \u2192 "Progressni tozalash" tugmasi yoki o'yinni o'chirib tashlash telefoningizdagi barcha o'yin ma'lumotlarini o'chiradi. Statistika nomsiz bo'lgani uchun uni sizning shaxsingizga bog'lab bo'lmaydi; o'yin qayta o'rnatilsa, unga yangi tasodifiy identifikator beriladi. Google Play'dagi xaridlar tarixi Google hisobingizda qoladi.

If this policy changes, the new text will be published on this page and in the game, with a new date.
